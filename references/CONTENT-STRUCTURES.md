## HOOK ARCHITECTURES

### The Triple Hook (Chris Chung)
Layer three hooks simultaneously for maximum stopping power:
1. **Written hook** — On-screen text (e.g., "The #1 mistake killing your reach")
2. **Verbal hook** — Spoken words (different from the text — gives TWO reasons to stay)
3. **Visual hook** — Pattern interrupt (movement, props, unexpected visual)
- **When:** Every Reel. Non-negotiable.

### Super Hook (Chris Chung)
The SECOND line after your hook. Establishes WHY they should listen to YOU:
1. Hook grabs attention (0-1s)
2. Super Hook establishes authority (1-3s): "As someone who's grown 50 accounts..." or "After studying 10,000 viral videos..."
- **When:** Educational or authority content

### ⭐ The Iceberg Effect Hook
Focus on a TINY detail everyone ignores → reveal massive consequences:
- "Does the amount of water you use to boil your pasta actually matter? Yes, it does."
- **When:** Educational content. Makes mundane topics feel urgent.

### ⭐ Contrarian Perspective Hook
Argue AGAINST the most common advice in your niche:
1. Identify the most common advice
2. Target your audience's hidden frustration with that advice
3. Argue the opposite
- Example: If everyone says "post every day" → "Stop posting every day"
- **When:** Thought leadership. Gets comments (agreement + disagreement).

### Comparison Hook
Pit two things against each other:
- **Price:** "₱50 vs ₱300 vs ₱1,000 planner"
- **Test:** "Spiral binding vs stapled binding — which lasts?"
- **Product:** "Expensive machine vs cheap alternative"
- **When:** Product content, reviews, educational

### ⭐ The 100-View vs Million-View Hook (Awareness Levels)
A bad hook makes only ONE person care. A viral hook makes ANYONE care:
- **Unaware audience:** "The only difference between a supermodel and a normal person is tongue posture" (anyone can relate)
- **Problem-aware:** "If you want a dog who will quietly sit..." (only dog owners care)
- Widen the hook to hit the UNAWARE audience for maximum reach
- **When:** Trying to break out of your niche bubble

### Hook Type Catalog
| Hook Type | Formula | Example |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| **Question** | Ask something they can't ignore | "Alam mo ba kung magkano talaga kinikita ko?" |
| **Bold Claim** | Counterintuitive statement | "You don't need expensive tools to start" |
| **Curiosity Gap** | Open a loop | "This one change doubled my sales..." |
| **Labeling** | Call out a specific group | "If you're a homeschool mom who..." |
| **Framework** | Introduce a proprietary system | "This is the 3-step method..." |
| **Story Leading** | Jump into narrative | "Last month, a customer messaged me..." |
| **Authority** | Borrow credibility | "Alex Hormozi always says..." |
| **Statistic** | Lead with a number | "93% of small businesses fail because..." |

## SCRIPT STRUCTURES (Short-Form)

### ⭐ Show Then Tell (15-30s)
1. **Second 0-1:** Show the END RESULT (finished product, improved metrics)
2. **Second 1-2:** One-line payoff ("This edit cut our cost per lead")
3. **Second 2-3:** Fast proof (on-screen stat or testimonial)
4. **Second 3-15:** Explain the process in 3 steps. No fluff.
5. **Second 15-30:** CTA with a specific reason
- **When:** Product demos, tutorials, process content

### ⭐ Myth, Truth, Move (30-45s)
1. **Second 0-2:** State a myth your audience believes
2. **Second 2-3:** Reveal the actual truth in ONE line
3. **Second 3-25:** Explain with ONE clear example
4. **Second 25-45:** Give their FIRST actionable next step
- **When:** Educational content that challenges assumptions

### Hook-Hold-Reward (any length)
1. **Hook (0-3s):** Immediate value, tension, or intrigue
2. **Hold (middle):** Deliver on the hook's promise. Fast-paced.
3. **Reward (end):** Payoff that encourages like/follow/share
- **When:** Universal — works for any content type

### Micro-Story Arc
1. **Hook:** Unexplained visual or question (open loop)
2. **Journey:** Walk through the process/story
3. **Resolution:** Close the loop with the reveal
- **When:** Process videos, transformations, before/after

### ⭐ Full Reel Anatomy (Chris Chung)
1. **Hook** (0-1s) — Pain + Benefit + Curiosity
2. **Super Hook** (1-3s) — Credibility/social proof
3. **Context** (3-7s) — Set the scene, explain the problem
4. **The Dance** (7-20s) — Loop context and conflict, build tension
5. **Re-hooks** mid-video — Reset attention with new curiosity
6. **Value/Climax** — Deliver the promised insight
7. **Re-loop** — Add "But..." near end to loop retention
8. **CTA** — Natural next step
- **When:** Any Reel over 30 seconds

## SCRIPT STRUCTURES (Long-Form / YouTube)

### HEIT Framework
1. **Hook** (0-15s) — Set up the video's promise. Clear for new viewers.
2. **Explain** — State the problem or question
3. **Illustrate** — Story or analogy that makes it real
4. **Teach** — Actionable lesson they can use today
- **When:** Tutorial/educational YouTube videos

### ⭐ 15-to-30 Minute Structure (Caleb Ralston)
Don't cram 5 concepts into one video. Teach ONE concept with FIVE stories/examples:
1. **Intro** → Context → Principle
2. **Story 1** → Tactic/Action → Example
3. **Story 2** → Tactic/Action → Example
4. **Story 3** → Tactic/Action → Example
5. **Link to next video**
- **When:** Deep-dive YouTube content

### ⭐ Value Front-Loading (Caleb Ralston)
Compress more usable value into the FIRST 60 seconds than most creators put in their entire video. Viewers assume the rest will be equally dense → they stay.
- **When:** Any YouTube video. The first minute determines retention.

## STORYTELLING STRUCTURES

### ⭐ 6-Step "My Story" (goes viral almost every time)
1. **The Intro:** State the problem you were feeling ("One year ago, I was broke...")
2. **The Inflection Point:** The exact pain moment ("I had to borrow money for groceries...")
3. **The Rising Action:** Failed solutions ("I tried freelancing, MLM, online selling...")
4. **The Climax:** The solution you found — YOUR product/service
5. **The Falling Action:** The results ("I now earn 6 figures from home...")
6. **The Resolution:** CTA ("Follow for more" / "I made it my mission to help moms like me")
- **When:** Founder story, brand story, course launch

### Year-by-Year Story
1. Write one significant event per year of your life
2. Find one photo per time period
3. Stitch them together with voiceover
- **When:** Personal brand building, milestone content

### ⭐ Three-Part Brand Story (Caleb Ralston)
Skip the basic "problem-transformation-outcome":
1. **The Catalyst:** Why your brand exists. The gap you saw.
2. **The Core Truth:** The contrarian conviction that sets you apart
3. **The Proof:** Consistent actions and case studies
- **When:** Brand positioning content

### 4 Founder Videos (every founder needs these)
1. **Founder Story:** How you built the business from zero
2. **Product Story:** How you prototyped/created your product
3. **Product Breakdown:** Step-by-step how it works for customers
4. **Day in the Life:** Documenting your founder day
- **When:** Building personal brand + business credibility

### ⭐ Share the Messy Middle (Caleb Ralston)
People connect more with failures than highlights. Share mistakes + lessons learned.
- **When:** Trust-building content. Works great in stories.

## AD / SELLING STRUCTURES

### PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve)
1. **Problem:** Introduce specific pain point
2. **Agitate:** Poke at the pain — make it emotional
3. **Solve:** Present product as the clear solution
- **When:** Direct response ads, product launches

### Before-After-Bridge
1. **Before:** Visualize their current struggle
2. **After:** Show the transformation
3. **Bridge:** How YOUR product gets them there
- **When:** Course/product ads, testimonial content

### Hook-Story-Offer
1. **Hook:** Curiosity-inducing opening
2. **Story:** Entertaining, compelling narrative
3. **Offer:** Transition story into product offer
- **When:** Warm audience selling, retargeting ads

### ⭐ WHO-WHY-OFFER-ACTION
1. **Who:** Call out your specific audience ("Homeschool moms in PH...")
2. **Why It Matters:** Show how it solves their specific problem
3. **The Offer:** Product + social proof + scarcity
4. **Call to Action:** Simple, direct, frictionless
- **When:** Cold audience ads

### ⭐ Benefit-Caveat
1. **Start Positive:** Discuss a desirable goal within reach
2. **Make Personal:** Link it directly to the viewer
3. **Introduce Caveat:** "But there's one thing stopping you..."
4. **Promote Action:** How to overcome the obstacle
- **When:** Educational selling, webinar promos

## RETENTION & EDITING TECHNIQUES

### ⭐ Curiosity Stacking (Chris Chung)
Open NEW loops before closing old ones:
- "Before I met Mark..." (opens loop about Mark)
- Add context, delay payoff (viewer MUST stay)
- Close the loop at the very end
- **Rule:** Always have at least one open loop

### ⭐ Re-hooks (Chris Chung)
Mid-video attention resets:
- "But here's where it gets interesting..."
- "The part nobody talks about is..."
- Place every 7-10 seconds in short-form

### ⭐ The Zeigarnik Effect Opening
Show ALL elements of your video at the very beginning:
- "I'm going to test these 5 machines and one of them shocked me"
- Brain MUST stick around to see which one
- **When:** List content, comparison content, testing content

### ⭐ 1.1x Speed Hack (Chris Chung)
Throw final video into CapCut and bump speed to 1.1x. Instantly snappier without sounding unnatural.

### ⭐ Audio Sweetening (Briar Cochran)
Speed up video 1.05x + slight pitch increase. Subconsciously prevents boredom.

### Trim All Dead Space
Manually cut every millisecond of silence/breathing between lines.

### ⭐ Active Visual Engagement (Chris Chung)
DO something physical while talking: pour a drink, walk, make food, organize supplies.
Keeps viewer visually engaged during educational content.

## ALGORITHM EXPLOITATION

### ⭐ Trial Reels Exploit (Chris Chung)
Most people use Trial Reels to test NEW content. Do the OPPOSITE:
- Take your BEST-PERFORMING, already viral reels
- Repost as Trial Reels (only shown to non-followers)
- Proven content goes viral AGAIN without annoying existing audience

### ⭐ Green Screen Clone Hack (Chris Chung)
Instagram patched reposting as Trial Reels. Bypass:
- Record value over green screen
- Swap background image 7 times = 7 "new" videos
- Same audio/value, different visual = passes duplicate detector

### ⭐ B-Roll Lead Magnet Printing (Briar Cochran)
1. Record 100 simple B-roll clips (outfit checks, mirror selfies, walking)
2. Add pain-point text hooks ("If you aren't doing X, what are you doing?")
3. Tell them to comment a keyword for a free guide
4. Post 10-20 per day → auto-triggers DM funnel
- **When:** Lead generation at scale

### ⭐ "Yap Fun Fact" Sentiment Test (Briar Cochran)
1. Ask ChatGPT for 20 fun facts about your niche
2. Record yourself saying "Fun fact..." for all 20
3. Post as Trial Reels
4. Whichever gets most views = what your audience ACTUALLY wants
- **When:** Topic validation before investing in full production

### ⭐ Comment Farming (Briar Cochran)
NOT rage-baiting. Plant passive visual anomalies:
- Walk with unzipped bag, gym bag on car roof
- Viewers flood comments to point it out
- Reply to EVERY comment with a QUESTION → doubles comment count

### Zero Hashtags, SEO Keywords (Chris Chung)
Hashtags are dead for growth. Instead:
- SAY your target keywords out loud in the video
- Write keywords naturally in caption
- Instagram's AI transcribes audio and reads captions for distribution

## META-STRATEGIES

### ⭐ 4-on-4 Validation (Sam Gaudet)
Before creating, find:
- **4 outlier videos** on the same topic
- That achieved **4x average views**
- Within the last **4 months**
- If you can't find them → don't make the content

### 70/20/10 Content Split
- **70%** — Proven formats (>1x multiplier historically)
- **20%** — Iterations (tweak one element of proven format)
- **10%** — Pure experiments (expect flops, celebrate learnings)

### CCN Fit (Topic Validation)
Before creating, check:
- **Core** (buyers): Does this serve them?
- **Casual** (watchers): Does this engage them?
- **New** (strangers): Is this accessible to them?

### ⭐ The Accordion Method (Sam Gaudet)
1. **Expand:** Post high volume across many formats/topics to gather data
2. **Contract:** Cut everything below 1x multiplier. Double down on winners.
3. **Repeat:** Expand again with variations of winners

### ⭐ Eye of Sauron (Caleb Ralston)
Pick 3 platforms but focus ALL innovation energy on ONE at a time. Others on maintenance mode. Once the first is dialed in, rotate focus.

### ⭐ ICP Subtext (Sam Gaudet)
Put a specific person's name + description at the top of every script:
- "Frustrated Grace: Homeschool mom, wants to earn from home, overwhelmed by tech"
- Forces the talent to speak to ONE person, not an audience

### ⭐ Wrapping Paper Library (Caleb Ralston)
Never create titles/thumbnails day-of. Constantly screenshot outlier packaging from ANY niche. When you have a concept, search this library for the best "wrapper."

### ⭐ Containers (Sam Gaudet)
Turn real life into content without scripting:
- During events/activities, implement light pre-production rules
- Brief audience to ask short, contextual questions
- Clip authentic answers into viral content

## PRODUCTION SYSTEMS

### ⭐ The 5 M's (Sam Gaudet)
1. **Model:** Find proven concepts (outlier research)
2. **Map:** Plan content buckets and calendar
3. **Make:** Create "containers" for batch filming
4. **Multiply:** Distribute across platforms (Lake Method, not waterfall)
5. **Measure:** Track multipliers and followers added per video

### ⭐ Lake Method vs Waterfall (Sam Gaudet)
**Old way (Waterfall):** YouTube video → chop into shorts for every platform
**New way (Lake):** All raw materials go into central pool. Each platform's owner "fishes" for clips that work on THEIR algorithm.

### Batch Filming System (Sam Gaudet / Dan Martell)
- 12 hours filming per MONTH (3 hours/day × 4 days)
- Film first thing in morning (highest energy)
- Pre-production: topics, hooks, and outlines BEFORE filming day
- 2-video buffer: always have 2 videos ahead to prevent burnout

### ⭐ Waterfall Distribution 2.0 (Caleb Ralston)
Don't chop long-form into identical shorts. Mine for self-contained "golden moments" and repackage them NATIVELY for each platform.
